# Contributing to AudiForm 2

Thank you for helping. Two rules come before everything else.

## 1. Clean-room only

Write your contribution yourself. Do not copy, translate or closely
paraphrase code from Audiform 1, from any editor Audiform 1 was forked
from, or from any project whose licence or credit terms AudiForm 2 has not
taken on. Learning how a feature behaves is fine; carrying its code over
is not. Third-party libraries need a licence compatible with GPL-3.0 and
an entry in `ATTRIBUTION.md`.

- it is your own work, or you have the right to submit it;
- it is licensed to everyone under GPL-3.0-or-later; and
- you also grant the AudiForm 2 project lead a perpetual, worldwide,
  royalty-free, irrevocable licence to use, modify, sublicense and
  relicense your contribution under any other terms, including a future

You keep the copyright in your contribution. Add a `Signed-off-by:` line
to your commits to confirm the above.

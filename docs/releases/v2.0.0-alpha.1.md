# AudiForm 2.0.0-alpha.1

> **⚠️ Highly experimental.** This is the first public alpha of AudiForm 2.
> Expect bugs, rough edges and changes between builds. Keep backups of
> sessions you care about. **Please report any problem** at
> https://github.com/nwh212/AudiForm2/issues (or Help > Report a Problem
> in the app). Include the steps, and a session file or screenshot if you can.

AudiForm 2 is the all-Rust sequel to [Audiform](https://github.com/nwh212/Audiform):
a fast, themeable audio editor with a Waveform editor, a Multitrack editor,
effects, VST3 hosting and local AI stem separation.

## Download

- `AudiForm2-2.0.0-alpha.1-windows-x64.exe`: a single portable program with
  ASIO support. No installer is needed. It keeps its settings in
  `%APPDATA%\Audiform27`.

## Highlights

- **Multitrack**
  - Audio, bus and MIDI tracks, sends, sidechains, a mixer, and a master with pan, mono and invert.
  - Clip gain with dB readouts, and straight, curved and bell (S-curve) fades.
  - Volume and pan lines on every clip, plus track and plug-in automation.
  - **Pre-render** for heavy tracks.
  - Edits made while playing no longer restart effects, so there are no volume dips.
- **Effects rack**
  - 16 slots on tracks, buses, clips and the master.
  - Built-in presets.
  - The Audiform effects, including Amp Model (`.nam`), Tube Preamp, Tube Master (any channel count), Opto Leveler and Vox Strip.
  - A 7-band Parametric EQ.
- **VST3 hosting**: plug-ins run outside the app, at audio priority.
- **Project files**
  - `.audfrm` round-trips byte for byte with Audiform.
  - `.sesx` brings over clip and track effects, I/O, automation and plug-in settings, with built-in effects mapped to AudiForm 2's own. `.sesx` export is included.
  - `.aup3` import is read-only.
- **Waveform editor**
  - Mute channels by clicking their letters.
  - Spectral box selections that play only the frequencies inside them, looping.
- **AI stem separation**: through the new separation engine 1.0, downloaded on demand.
- **Audio**: WASAPI and **ASIO**.
- **Themes**: 13 built in, including the default Zero Point and **Studio Lite**, a flat theme for low-end computers.

## Known limitations

- Windows is the only tested platform.
- ASIO playback and recording have had little testing so far.
- Multichannel AI separation isn't available yet; it runs on stereo and mono.
- Some features from Audiform 1 are still missing. See `docs/parity.md`.

# AudiForm 2.0.1

> **⚠️ Highly experimental.** AudiForm 2 is still an early build. Expect
> bugs, rough edges and changes between builds. Keep backups of sessions
> you care about. **Please report any problem** at
> https://github.com/AudiFormer/AudiForm2/issues (or Help > Report a Problem
> in the app). Include the steps, and a session file or screenshot if you can.

## Download

- `AudiForm2-2.0.1-windows-x64.exe`: a single portable program with ASIO
  support. No installer is needed. It keeps its settings in
  `%APPDATA%\Audiform2`.

## New

- **MIDI instruments**
  - MIDI tracks can play **VST3 instruments**. The instrument menu (the ▾ arrow) sorts them by maker and kind, and has a search box.
  - Clicking the instrument's name opens its window.
  - Keys from a MIDI keyboard play the armed track live, through its effects, in either editor.
  - Sustain, sostenuto, mod wheel and pitch bend reach the instrument.
- **Built-in synth**
  - The default tone is kept, and can now be shaped with real synth controls in an editor window.
  - Eight models, inspired by classic synths: Default, Mono Ladder, Poly Chorus, Acid Line, Brass Poly, FM Keys, String Machine and Sync Lead.
- **Multitrack**
  - Overlapping clips work as in Audiform 1: a clip on top covers the clips under it, and those play through only under its fades.
  - Automatic and linked crossfades.
  - Overlaps are drawn as in Audiform 1.
  - Clip edges can be dragged to trim, on audio and MIDI clips.
  - Snapping takes the nearer of a clip's start or end, and a line shows where it snapped.
  - Bar and beat lines appear only on MIDI tracks.
  - Double-clicking a clip opens the part it plays in the Waveform editor.
  - Recording draws the take behind the playhead.
- **Properties panel**: Info, Basic Settings, Stretch and Source Channel Routing.
- **Effects**
  - Delay and Echo has Digital, Tape, Analog and Simple types.
  - Vox Strip and Reverb follow their Audiform layouts.
  - The rack's Add Effect menu matches the Effects menu.
  - Double-clicking a rack slot opens the effect's window.
- **Formats**
  - Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus (Atmos beds), HE-AAC and AC-4 open through Windows' own decoders.
  - Multitrack `.mogg` files with more than 8 channels open.
  - Anything else falls back to an installed ffmpeg.
- **Memory**
  - Large sessions use much less RAM.
  - Pre-renders stream from disk as 24-bit lossless chunks.
- **Podcast and spoken word**: a new **Spoken Word** workspace. The Default and Music layouts are unchanged.
  - Speech and repair effects: Speech Leveler, Breath Gate, Click Repair, Clip Repair, Noise Reducer, Hiss Reducer and Room Reducer.
  - Capture Noise Print and Reduce Noise with Print.
  - Diagnostics panel:
    - finds pauses and dead air, clicks and mouth noise, and clipped peaks;
    - deletes, shortens, repairs or marks them, one undo step per fix.
  - Loudness panel: live LUFS, loudness range and true peak, measured against podcast, streaming, broadcast and audiobook targets.
  - Multitrack > Spoken Word:
    - Auto Duck, Speaker Balance and Strip Silence;
    - Add Podcast Tracks: Host and Guest with a clean-up chain, Music Bed and Sound Effects.
  - Spoken-word rack presets, such as Voice Clean-Up, Remote Guest Clean-Up and Audiobook Narration.
  - Marker kinds:
    - markers can be Chapter, Ad Slot, Social Clip or Range;
    - Markers > Export writes a chapter list, CSV or podcast chapters JSON;
    - FLAC exports carry the chapters.
- **Visualizer**: a new **TV Dancer** look.

## Fixed

- MIDI notes could get stuck while adding or dragging them. Play and Stop now also silence any held preview notes.
- Clicking a popup over the piano roll or the tracks no longer places notes or clicks through to what's under it.
- MIDI keyboards now play in the Waveform editor too. An input that won't open now says so.

## Known limitations

- Windows is the only tested platform.
- VST3 instruments always get 120 BPM, so tempo-synced arpeggiators and LFOs drift. Live playing ignores mute and solo.
- VST3 instruments have only been tested with a built-in test synth, not real plug-ins.
- 5.1 and 7.1 AAC files need ffmpeg installed.
- Some features from Audiform 1 are still missing. See `docs/parity.md`.
